# Documentation

## Write for the intended readers

- Supply the context readers need without relying on the editing conversation. Introduce relevant systems, terms, and constraints where they become necessary.
- Keep the document within its stated purpose and scope. Follow the user's requested style and preserve the established tone, terminology, and format when revising it.
- Keep modification logs and explanations of the editing process in the accompanying response unless requested as part of the document.
- Add references only when readers need them to verify a claim, understand the content, or use related material.
